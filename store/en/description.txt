Crash-test dummies. Kick scooters. No rules.

Race a pack of eight dummies down a busy highway and win any way you can. Slap the rider next to you, shove them into a passing car, swing a frying pan or a baseball bat, launch off ramps and land on someone's head. Get knocked off and you're back on your scooter a second later, but the pack won't wait.

- Five cups across five roads: green meadows, a desert, autumn woods, a snowy pass and a neon coast at dusk.
- Finish in the top three of a cup to move up. Win all five, then do it again in the Pro and Legend seasons.
- Grudges and nemeses: riders remember who knocked them off. A nemesis hunts you every race until you beat them for good, and there's a bounty on their head.
- Spend your prize money on top speed, handling and toughness, and unlock six rides, from a standard scooter to something that is probably illegal.
- Dodge traffic, construction zones and highway security, and take the dirt shortcuts if you dare.

Races take two to three minutes, and your career saves automatically.
